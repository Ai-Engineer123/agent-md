# Executive Meeting System Specification (MD Master Schedule)

## 1. Meeting Schedule & Time Window
- **Weekly Days**: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Saturday (First half only)
- **Time Window**: 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- **Exception**: Monthly OM & FM Meeting starts early at **8:00 AM** (First half).

## 2. Companies & Frequency Matrix

3. **CC** (City Coolies) — Twice a week (**Tuesday & Saturday**).

3. Site vacancy inspection

7. Employee verification
8. ESI & PF status (completed vs pending)

#### Admin Scope:
9. Uniform sales and deductions
10. Uniform stock & management
11. Shop sales
12. Current imprest holder cash in hand
13. Internal & external tickets audit
14. New site creations
15. Assets movement & transfer

18. Site-wise salary process
19. Payment collections
